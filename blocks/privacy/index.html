Plushvale Blocks privacy policy

Français (Canada)

Effective date: 2026-11-15

This policy explains information handling in Plushvale Blocks, published by GennenTech (GennenTech Software Corp.), Ontario, Canada. There is no game account or sign-in. Your saved game stays on your device; Google's advertising and consent services and Apple's App Store process information as explained below.

Who is responsible and how to contact us

GennenTech Software Corp., a corporation incorporated under the Canada Business Corporations Act, at 221 Queen Street, Kingston, Ontario K7K 1B4, Canada, is responsible for the personal information it handles. Our person in charge of protecting personal information is the Privacy Officer. Send privacy questions, requests or complaints to privacy@gennentech.com, addressed to the Privacy Officer.

What the app saves locally

Game progress and settings are saved on your device so you can resume play and keep your choices. We do not send these saves to a game server of ours. Device backups may retain or restore app data.

We have no player account database or separate gameplay analytics service in the app. This does not exclude Google's advertising diagnostics and measurement described below. Device backups may contain local app data under your Apple backup settings.

Google advertising and consent data

The free game uses Google AdMob interstitial ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may collect or process:

  • Coarse Location: your IP address and approximate location derived from it; the app does not request precise device location. Linked to your identity; not used for tracking. Purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics; App Functionality.
  • Device ID: app- or device-related identifiers, which may be linked to your identity; the app does not request ATT permission or use these identifiers for tracking. Purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics. Google's publisher first-party ID is disabled.
  • Product Interaction (such as taps and video views) and Advertising Data (such as ads shown). Both may be linked to your identity; the app does not use them for tracking. Product Interaction purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics; App Functionality. Advertising Data purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics.
  • Crash Data: not linked to your identity; not used for tracking; purpose: Analytics. Performance Data: linked to your identity; not used for tracking; purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics; App Functionality. Other Diagnostic Data: not linked to your identity; not used for tracking; purposes: Third-Party Advertising; Developer’s Advertising or Marketing; Analytics.
  • Consent status and privacy choices used to manage privacy messages and ad requests.

The app’s privacy manifest declares no tracking. The App Store privacy disclosures must be kept consistent with the app and its SDKs. Google uses this information for the purposes listed above, including advertising delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics and consent management. Having no game account does not make advertising data anonymous. Product Personalization and Other Purposes are not declared purposes.

Third parties with whom we share personal information provide protection at least equivalent to this policy and Apple's App Review Guidelines.

Why information is processed and consent

We use Google AdMob to fund the free game with ads, measure ad delivery, limit repeated ads and help prevent fraud. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or read the IDFA for tracking. It requests non-personalised ads. Google UMP manages consent messages and choices where required; ad requests depend on the consent status returned by Google. Non-personalised ads can still involve identifiers and device storage. Google’s publisher first-party ID is disabled. You can review available privacy choices in Settings → More → Privacy Choices where the consent framework requires it. Local saves support gameplay; support correspondence is used to handle your request. Where EU or UK data protection law applies, we rely on consent where legally required, contract for processing necessary to provide the game and purchased benefits, legitimate interests in answering support requests and investigating problems, and legal obligations for statutory privacy requests.

See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google's explanation for partner apps and Google's Privacy Policy. Advertising measurement by Google is separate from our local game saves.

Your choices

Open Settings → More → Privacy Choices to review or change the choices Google makes available where the consent framework requires it. The row appears when UMP reports that privacy options are required; its absence does not mean no information is processed. If the form fails to open or you need help, email the Privacy Officer at privacy@gennentech.com. Changing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing.

The optional one-time Buy this game purchase removes ads. Once ownership is verified, the app stops making new ad requests and clears pending ads. This does not erase information already held by Google. To report an inappropriate ad, use Report an ad in Settings or email support@gennentech.com; see support.

Apple purchases

Apple handles payments and purchase records under App Store & Privacy. StoreKit supplies verified transaction and entitlement information so the app can enable paid benefits and restore them. Ownership is refreshed from StoreKit; we do not operate a purchase server or receive payment card details. Restoring a purchase restores paid benefits, not deleted saved games.

Support messages and ad reports

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use these to answer you, investigate a problem or handle a privacy complaint. Zoho hosts our support email and processes messages for delivery and storage. For support and US state privacy rights requests, email support@gennentech.com. An ad report may be shared with Google to investigate the ad; include only details needed to identify it. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain on your device until replaced during play or removed with the app. To remove local app data, choose Delete App, rather than Offload App. A device backup can retain or restore older data. We cannot retrieve or erase your device's save files remotely.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request, then deleted. You can request earlier deletion using our privacy contact. Google and Apple control retention of information in their services under their linked privacy notices; deleting this app does not delete their records or your App Store purchase.

Your rights and Quebec privacy protections

Quebec's private-sector privacy law (as amended by Law 25) applies to our handling of personal information. You can write to our privacy officer to request access or correction, withdraw consent subject to legal limits, request deletion where applicable, or request a structured, commonly used copy of eligible computerised information collected from you. We may need information to verify your identity. We respond in writing to access and correction requests within 30 days of receipt, as Quebec law requires.

Our privacy officer is the contact for complaints and questions about how information is handled, retained or deleted. For information held by Google or Apple, their linked privacy notices provide request channels; you may also contact us for help. You can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as applicable. Quebec law also requires safeguards appropriate to the information, a confidentiality-incident register, and notice to the regulator and affected people when an incident presents a risk of serious injury. These duties, and the transfer-assessment duty above, are separate from publishing this policy.

Children and age ratings

Plushvale Blocks is a general-audience puzzle game. Check the App Store age rating for your region; an age rating is not consent to collect a child's information. Our release configuration calls for Apple's Declared Age Range where required and for the available platform age signal to be checked when the app opens. The signal is read on device only; it is passed to Google solely as child-directed or under-age-of-consent advertising flags. We do not ask a separate age question or operate an age-data server. An under-13 signal calls for child-directed ad treatment. Known children must receive no personalised ads or tracking; other under-age-of-consent signals must receive the applicable protections. If a lawful restricted advertising path cannot be established, no ads are to be shown to that player. These are the release's required protections, not a claim that every runtime path has been verified. Google's G (general-audience) ad content rating is requested, but cannot guarantee every ad's suitability. Parents or guardians with concerns about a child's information or an ad should contact our privacy officer so we can investigate and handle any applicable access or deletion request.
