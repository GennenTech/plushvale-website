How do I play?

Drag felt pieces onto the board. Complete a row or a column to clear it and make room. Classic builds your score as the challenge grows; Blitz challenges you to clear lines before the countdown runs out; Puzzles asks you to clear a board with a fixed supply of pieces (turn on Relaxed puzzles in Settings to play without its timer); Daily Puzzle is the day's puzzle, one first attempt that counts. Tap How to Play in Settings for a short tour.

What does Buy this game include?

Tap Buy this game on Home. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Remove ads. Get 5 Continues per Classic game and 3 per Blitz game. Progress beyond Puzzle Level 3 to Levels 4–8. Buying does not skip levels. Puzzles and Daily Puzzle have no Continues. See Licence & purchases.

How do I restore my purchase?

On Home, open Buy this game, then choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the account that bought the game (your Apple Account on iOS, your Google Account on Google Play) and connected to the internet. Restore checks your paid benefits with the App Store or Google Play; it does not bring back deleted game progress.

What if Buy or Restore does not work?

Both need an internet connection. If the app cannot reach the App Store or Google Play, check your connection and try again. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy on iOS) or is pending in Google Play, wait for it to complete. If it still fails, email us the message shown in the app.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com; eligibility is decided through that process and the law that applies to you. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases ourselves. For purchases made on Google Play, Google Play's refund policies apply, and you can also email us about the purchase.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Open Settings → More → Privacy Choices where the consent framework requires it. It reopens Google's privacy form to review or change available choices. The row appears when UMP reports that privacy options are required; its absence does not mean no information is processed. The app requests non-personalised ads; on iOS, it does not request Apple's tracking permission. Google's publisher first-party ID is disabled. On iOS, the release requires no personalised ads or tracking for known children, and no ads if a lawful restricted ad path cannot be established. On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's settings. If the form fails to open, email our Privacy Officer at privacy@gennentech.com. See our privacy policy for data, retention, rights and contact details.

How do I report an ad?

Use Report an ad in Settings (look under More), or email support@gennentech.com. Tell us the approximate time, advertiser name if visible, and what was inappropriate or misleading. A screenshot can help; remove personal information first. You do not need to tap the ad. We can use the details to investigate with Google. The app requests Google's G ad content rating; please report any ad that seems unsuitable.

How do I reset my progress?

On iOS, delete the app and reinstall it (choose Delete App, not Offload App, which keeps data). On Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. This removes local progress and settings. Restoring a device backup may bring old progress back; on Android, backup can restore saved game files when the app is installed again, so reinstalling may not start fresh. Deleting the app or clearing its storage does not cancel your purchase: tap Restore Purchases afterwards.
