Licence & purchases

Updated: 2026-11-15

Plushvale Blocks is provided by GennenTech (GennenTech Software Corp.), Ontario, Canada. This page explains, in plain words, how the game is licensed and what the purchase does. It is not a custom EULA and not an additional set of contractual terms.

Your app licence

Your use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. GennenTech is the application provider. The linked EULA sets out your licence and its conditions.

Play free, or buy once

The free game shows ads between games. "Buy this game" is an optional one-time App Store purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads. Get 5 Continues per Classic game and 3 per Blitz game. Progress beyond Puzzle Level 3 to Levels 4–8. Buying does not skip levels. Puzzles and Daily Puzzle have no Continues. The app shows the current price in your region before you buy. Apple processes the payment.

Restore and refunds

Tap Buy this game on Home, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase and connected to the internet. Restore checks your paid benefits; it does not recover deleted game progress. If you need help, contact support.

Refunds for App Store purchases are requested through Apple's purchase support; eligibility is decided through that process and the law that applies to you. We cannot issue refunds ourselves. Nothing on this page limits your mandatory consumer rights.

Your progress

Progress, stars, unlocked scenes and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app, moving to another device without a backup, or a device failure can lose that progress, and we cannot restore it. Restore Purchases checks your paid benefits with the App Store. Purchases, restoring a purchase, ads and consent updates need an internet connection.

Ads

Ads in the free version are provided by Google and its advertising partners; we do not choose or endorse individual ads. Your privacy choices and what Google processes are explained in our privacy policy. To report an ad that seems wrong, see the support page.

The game includes art created with AI assistance.

Updates

We update the app to fix things, adjust balance, add scenes or modes, or meet platform rules. When something on this page changes, we update the date at the top.
